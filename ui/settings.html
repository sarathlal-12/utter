Welcome to Utter

Dictate into any app, fully offline. Nothing you say leaves this computer.

  1. Click into any text box: email, chat, docs, code.
  2. Hold Ctrl+Win and speak.
  3. Let go. Clean text appears at your cursor and stays on your clipboard.

For long dictation, press Space while holding the keys to go hands-free. Press the keys again to finish. Esc cancels.
